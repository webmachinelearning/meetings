### Beyond WebMCP

>Goal: Per [The Agentic Web breakout discussion](https://gist.github.com/yoavweiss/211c446c914a5abf03548652e31de9a8) ([slides](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1UO_UwtxtxZHqWH1B3Ou7_6Ftmd-X_uPb/view)), advance the proposed Agentic Web WG's [tentative deliverables](https://w3c.github.io/charter-drafts/2026/agenticweb-wg.html#incubation) issue discussions into explainer form to clarify the problem statements, proposed solutions, use cases, and technical considerations.

#### Agent Identity

Mechanisms for sites and agents to mutually convey and negotiate identity, attribution of agent-originated interaction.

Related issues:

- ⨀ Agent identity https://github.com/webmachinelearning/webmcp/issues/54
- ⨀ Attribution of agent-originated events https://github.com/webmachinelearning/webmcp/issues/365 and https://github.com/w3c/strategy/issues/576#issuecomment-6413882762

#### Agent Delegation

Mechanisms for users to grant agents authority to act on their behalf on a site to take actions. Understand implications for different interaction modes, including enterprise use cases and requirements.

Related issues:

- ⨀ Action-specific permissions https://github.com/webmachinelearning/webmcp/issues/44
- ⨀ Agents as separately authorized clients https://github.com/webmachinelearning/webmcp/issues/54
- ⨀ Scope https://github.com/w3c/charter-drafts/issues/906

#### Agent Interaction Measurement

APIs for pages to measure in-field agent interaction performance, reliability.

Related issues:

- ⨀ Real-user measurement https://github.com/webmachinelearning/webmcp/issues/186

#### Agentic Interaction Modes

Non-normative guidelines for agentic interaction in different modes (browser, extension, automation, headless) using tools, DOM/a11y tree, screenshots, synthesized input. Implications for security, privacy, and user experience.
